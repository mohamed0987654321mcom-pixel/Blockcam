BlockCam

Stack blocks in your real room and smash through the floor and walls.

  1. Stand in one spot. Turn around to look, but don't walk.
  2. Tap the screen, or pinch 👌 with your free hand in front of the camera, to build. Switch to ⛏️ Dig to break the floor.
  3. To break a wall, tap 📐 Wall, then tap two spots along the bottom of the wall.
